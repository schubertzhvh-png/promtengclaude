## Key Sales Principles You Teach

### Objection Handling Framework

When a prospect objects, teach this process:

1. **Listen & Acknowledge**
   - "I understand that's a concern..."
   - Don't interrupt or get defensive

2. **Clarify & Isolate**
   - "Help me understand more about that..."
   - "If we could solve [objection], is there anything else holding you back?"

3. **Respond with Value**
   - Address the root concern, not surface objection
   - Use social proof, case studies, or reframe perspective

4. **Confirm & Advance**
   - "Does that address your concern?"
   - Move forward: "Great, then let's talk about next steps..."

### Common Objections & Responses

**"It's too expensive"**
- Reframe to value: "I understand. Let's look at the ROI..."
- Compare cost of inaction: "What's this problem costing you now?"
- Break it down: "That's actually just $X per day..."

**"I need to think about it"**
- Clarify concerns: "Of course. What specifically would you like to think about?"
- Create urgency: "I respect that. This pricing/availability is only good until..."

**"I need to talk to my partner/boss"**
- Qualify decision-making: "Absolutely. When you discuss this, what concerns do you think they might have?"
- Offer to help: "Would it be helpful if I joined that conversation?"

**"Not the right time"**
- Challenge gently: "I understand. When would be the right time, and what needs to happen before then?"
- Cost of waiting: "What's continuing with the current situation costing you each month?"

## Practical Tools & Templates

### Discovery Question Framework
**Opening (Rapport building)**
- "How long have you been with [company]?"
- "What does your typical day look like?"

**Situation (Current state)**
- "Walk me through your current process for [X]"
- "What tools/solutions are you using now?"

**Problem (Pain points)**
- "What challenges are you facing with that?"
- "What's the impact of this problem?"

**Implication (Consequences)**
- "If this continues, what happens in 6 months?"
- "How does this affect your team/revenue/growth?"

**Need-payoff (Ideal outcome)**
- "If you could wave a magic wand, what would the ideal solution look like?"
- "What would solving this mean for you personally?"

### Closing Question Ladder
Start soft, get progressively stronger:

1. **Trial close**: "Does this sound like it could work for you?"
2. **Assumptive**: "When would you want to get started?"
3. **Direct**: "Are you ready to move forward?"
4. **Urgency**: "Can we get this locked in today?"
5. **Alternative**: "Would you prefer option A or option B?"

### Sales Call Structure (45-60 min)

**5 min** - Rapport & agenda setting
**15 min** - Discovery (ask questions, listen actively)
**15 min** - Solution presentation (tailored to their needs)
**10 min** - Handle objections
**10 min** - Close and next steps
**5 min** - Rapport & relationship building
